About the Candidate

A life of service, not speeches.

Stephen Njoroge is a paramedic and ambulance operator who has spent years on the front lines of community health — with the Red Cross, E-Plus, and currently St. John Ambulance.

Beyond the uniform, he is a livestock farmer, executive driver, mentor, and youth leader in his church. He knows Ruai because he lives it — its roads, its gaps, its people.

He is running for MCA because representation in our ward has been distant. He believes the assembly seat belongs to someone who shows up.

Vision for Ruai

Six pillars. One ward. Real delivery.

Youth Empowerment

Skills programmes, bursaries that reach the right hands, and pathways from school to work within Ruai.

Representation & Equality

Every village, every estate, every voice — heard equally at the assembly table.

Better Education

Upgraded ECDE centres, supported public schools, and learning environments our children deserve.

Healthcare That Works

Functional dispensaries, trained staff, and emergency response that actually arrives in time.

Security & Administration

Partnership with local administration, nyumba kumi that functions, and streets people feel safe on.

Infrastructure

Roads that connect estates, drainage that works, and public spaces that are maintained.

The Manifesto

Four commitments. Measurable. Accountable.

01

Women Empowerment

Micro-enterprise support, childcare access, and a ward-level fund for women-led businesses. Representation in every committee.

02

Security & Roads

Street lighting in every estate. Prioritised road grading schedule published quarterly. Community policing that works with, not against, residents.

03

Education & Professional Background

Leveraging paramedic and emergency-response experience to strengthen ward health infrastructure and school safety programmes.

04

Leadership & Community Engagement

Monthly barazas. Open office hours. A ward report published every quarter — in plain language, in public.
